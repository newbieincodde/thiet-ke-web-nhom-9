Nutrition that adapts to different needs.

OVERVIEW

What are special nutrition needs?

Special nutrition needs refer to situations where nutritional requirements may differ because of a particular stage of life, physical activity, or health condition.

KEY GROUPS

Who may have special nutrition needs?

Different situations can influence the way nutrition needs are understood and managed.

01
LIFE STAGE

Pregnancy

Pregnancy is a period when nutritional needs change to support the health and development of both the mother and baby.

02
ACTIVITY

Athletes

People who participate in regular or intensive physical activity may have different nutrition and hydration needs.

HEALTH

Health Conditions

Some health conditions can affect dietary needs. Nutrition advice for these situations should be based on reliable information and, when appropriate, professional guidance.

WHY IT MATTERS

Nutrition is not one-size-fits-all.

Recognizing different nutrition needs helps us understand that one approach may not work equally well for everyone.

01

Different Life Stages

Nutrition needs can change throughout life as the body grows and develops.

02

Physical Activity

Physical activity affects how the body uses energy and nutrients.

03

Individual Needs

Many factors influence nutrition needs, so individual differences matter.

GENERAL PRINCIPLES

Start with the basics.

Although nutritional needs vary, several general principles can support healthy eating patterns.

01

Personalized Diet

Mỗi thể trạng có một yêu cầu dưỡng chất riêng. Việc điều chỉnh tỷ lệ thức ăn phải dựa trên chỉ số cơ thể, giai đoạn tuổi và tình trạng bệnh lý thực tế.

02

Adequate Hydration

Water is an essential part of nutrition. Hydration needs can vary depending on activity and environment.

03

Professional Guidance

People with specific health or nutritional concerns may benefit from guidance from qualified professionals.
